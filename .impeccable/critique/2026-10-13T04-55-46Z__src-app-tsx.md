## Priority issues

### [P1] Manual deduction does not explain when to use it

Locations: src/InventoryCards.tsx:25; src/App.tsx:324. The action says ลดจำนวน and the editor only names the quantity. It never says this is for an already handwritten bill and creates no bill. An operator could deduct here and then create a system bill, deducting the same sale twice.

Fix: concise context in the expanded editor: ใช้เมื่อออกบิลเขียนมือแล้ว · ลดสต๊อกโดยไม่สร้างบิล. Offer a clear route to ทำบิล when a document is needed. Preserve direct deduction and Undo. Suggested command: $impeccable clarify.

### [P1] Catalog navigation silently discards unfinished work

Locations: src/App.tsx:169, :449, :546; src/CatalogForm.tsx:12. Fields live inside the mounted form; navigation and header Back unmount it. Category creation commits separately, so abandoning the model can leave an added category while losing model edits.

Fix: dirty-state protection with stay/leave-without-saving choices, or preserve catalog form state during ordinary in-app navigation. Do not add sale drafts. Suggested command: $impeccable harden.

### [P2] Making a bill loses product-finding tools

Location: src/App.tsx:730. Every active SKU appears in an unfiltered list. A larger variant catalog requires scanning; zero-stock items are selectable and failure is deferred until confirmation.

Fix: search/category recognition within the current sale page, clear availability, and prevent adding stock that cannot be sold. Preserve one-page selection, quantities, summary and confirmation. Suggested command: $impeccable shape.

### [P2] Existing-SKU editing foregrounds new-SKU creation

Locations: src/CatalogForm.tsx:35, :38. Step 2 starts with blank generator controls before existing variants. A price change requires unrelated generation controls and three steps.

Fix: show existing variants first; disclose the generator under เพิ่มสี / ไซซ์. Give simple price/photo/activation edits a compact path, retaining review for structural changes. Explain SKU alongside ตัวเลือกสินค้า. Suggested command: $impeccable distill.

### [P2] Phone bill preview is too small to verify comfortably

Locations: src/CashReceipt.tsx:6; src/App.tsx:971; src/simple-stock.css. At 320px the paper document scales to roughly 260px wide. The semantic table helps screen readers but not sighted users checking line names and totals.

Fix: preserve the paper form and exported layout; add expand/zoom or a readable collapsible item/total summary. Suggested command: $impeccable adapt.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

Stock and receipt have clear focus, related grouping and hierarchy. Catalog editing partially fails focus and one-decision-at-a-time; new-variant controls precede existing edits, weakening progressive disclosure. The unfiltered sale picker exceeds four simultaneous product choices as the catalog grows. Photos and summaries reduce recall demands, but product finding is weaker on sales. Receipt review and cancellation reassure with explicit resulting quantities. Ambiguous deduction, dense variant editing and tiny phone bill preview introduce uncertainty.

## Persona red flags

- Alex, frequent operator: sale list lacks search; history lacks filters; price edit takes three catalog steps.
- Jordan, first-time operator: must infer deduction versus bill creation and understand comma-separated SKU combinations.
- Sam, keyboard/screen-reader user: bill quantity labels omit product identity (src/App.tsx:624); oversell errors are not associated with affected quantity; inline deduction close lacks explicit focus restoration.
